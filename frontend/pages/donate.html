✅ Donation Tips

  • Food must be safe to eat — freshly cooked or well-packaged
  • Be accurate about quantities so recipients can plan
  • Mention allergens to help people with dietary restrictions
  • Set realistic expiry times — usually 4-6 hours for cooked food
  • Be available to answer pickup questions
  • Rate your recipients to build community trust

📋 Food Safety Guidelines

  • Cooked food: Safe for 4-6 hours at room temperature
  • Use clean, sealed containers for transport
  • Label containers with food type and time prepared
  • Refrigerated food can last up to 3-4 days
