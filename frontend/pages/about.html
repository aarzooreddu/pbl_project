Our Mission

Fighting Food Waste,
One Meal at a Time

India wastes approximately 68 million tonnes of food every year — while 195 million people go hungry. FoodSave bridges this gap by connecting food donors directly with communities in need.

The Problem

The Food Waste Crisis in India

India wastes enough food to feed its entire hungry population multiple times over. Wedding functions, restaurants, hotels, and events routinely discard massive quantities of perfectly safe, edible food.

This not only represents a massive humanitarian loss, but also a significant environmental problem — decomposing food waste in landfills produces methane, a potent greenhouse gas.
